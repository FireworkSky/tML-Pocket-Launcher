tModLoader
in your pocket.

Play modded Terraria on Android. Import your copy of the game, add your mods and arrange the touch controls to suit the way you play.

Installation options ↓ Updates

Android 9+ · ARM64 · In development

Import your game

Use a Steam ZIP archive, a GOG Linux installer or files from a copy of Terraria PE installed on your device.

Manage versions and mods

Keep several tModLoader versions side by side, import .tmod files, check dependencies and save mod presets.

Customize touch controls

Play with dual joysticks and touch menus. Move the controls in the layout editor to keep them within reach.

Install the launcher

You’ll need an ARM64 device running Android 9 or later and game files from your own copy of Terraria.

There’s no public APK yet. You can build the launcher from source.
